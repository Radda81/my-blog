Hello, and welcome! This is the first post on a blog built from nothing but **Markdown files**, a small Node.js build script, and plain HTML/CSS/JS on the front end — no frameworks, no bundlers.

## Why a blog

I wanted a place to write down things I learn, in a format that's:

- Easy to write (just Markdown)
- Fast to load (static HTML, no JavaScript framework)
- Comfortable to read on any device, in light or dark mode

## What to expect

Mostly short posts about things I'm working on or learning. Nothing fancy — just clear writing in a clean, readable layout.

Thanks for stopping by.
